## Overall Impression

The site does the hard, specific part well -- the anatomy map and the chatbot medical content are careful, non-generic work -- and then loses points on finishing details that do not cost much to fix: one set of pages got left behind in the recolor, the header has no landing spot between desktop and mobile, and the most important moment in the funnel (submitting the form) hands off to a bare OS dialog. None of the priority issues below require rethinking the design; they are inconsistencies in a system that is otherwise coherent.

## What's Working

- Anatomy photo + accordion dual-coding. Letting a patient find where it hurts spatially (tap the photo) or by list, kept in sync, sidesteps clinical vocabulary a patient in pain should not have to know.
- The chatbot emergency-triage node. Concrete, correctly scoped red flags (visible deformity, bone through skin, loss of sensation, associated head/torso trauma) paired with an explicit go-to-the-ER-now instruction -- careful, liability-aware authoring, not filler.
- The predefined-graph chatbot architecture. Zero runtime generation for a medical practice is the right call, and the every-node-returns-to-main-menu rule is actually implemented correctly, not just documented.

## Priority Issues

[P0] Recursos articles are running the old color system
- Why it matters: The four article pages (protesis-rodilla-cadera.html, ligamento-cruzado-anterior.html, hernia-discal.html, fracturas-comunes.html) are exactly what a patient doing careful pre-surgery research reads most closely -- and these were built before the turquoise/navy recolor and never updated: they each carry their own embedded old-blue/gold palette and 4px-radius buttons, while index.html is fully on the new pill/glow system. A visitor clicking a resource card lands on what visually reads as a different, older site right as they are deciding whether to book.
- Fix: Port the new root variables, pill-button rules, and glow shadows from index.html's style block into all four article pages.
- Suggested command: /impeccable polish

[P1] Header has no tablet landing spot (roughly 769-900px)
- Why it matters: Measured directly -- at 769px the logo box balloons past the hard-coded 80px header height as the doctor's name wraps, bleeding into the hero below. The responsive rules jump straight from full desktop nav to the 768px hamburger breakpoint with nothing in between.
- Fix: Add an intermediate breakpoint (roughly 900-960px) that shrinks nav gap/font-size and hides the header's redundant CTA pill earlier, same pattern already used at 480px.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

[P1] Form success state is a native alert()
- Why it matters: The onsubmit handler calls preventDefault then a bare alert with the success message -- the payoff for the single highest-stakes action on the page, and it is an unstyled OS dialog with zero connection to the brand -- the worst moment in the emotional arc because it is the last one (violates the peak-end rule).
- Fix: Replace with an on-page confirmation panel reusing the existing check-icon component, plus a concrete next step (e.g. a callback window).
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P2] Chatbot symptom menu has 8 flat choices
- Why it matters: 8 ungrouped text buttons in a max-height 40% nested-scroll region of a 360px panel forces a scroll-within-scroll to reach the emergency question near position 7 -- the one spot in the whole graph where the choice-count guideline is actually violated without a content-driven excuse.
- Fix: Split into a first tier (joint pain / acute or sports injury / is this an emergency) that fans into the specific zone list.
- Suggested command: /impeccable distill

[P2] No aria-live on the chatbot message stream
- Why it matters: Screen-reader users get no announcement when a new bot bubble appears after clicking a choice -- the conversation silently updates for sighted users only.
- Fix: Add aria-live="polite" to the chatbot messages container.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit

## Minor Observations

- The hotspot mouseenter handler mutates persistent accordion state on hover alone -- should be a lighter visual cue, not a state change.
- The three placeholder stats render in the identical treatment as the one real figure (association count) -- nothing signals which are provisional, risking an un-replaced placeholder shipping unnoticed.
- No meta description or favicon on index.html.
- Undocumented shadow value on .btn-white -- DESIGN.md only describes it qualitatively as a soft black shadow.
- Several one-off font-sizes have no anchor in DESIGN.md's documented type scale at all -- not urgent, but the scale has drifted wider than what's written down.

## Questions to Consider

1. The recolor gave the homepage a bold, client-requested identity but left the four patient-education articles on the old palette -- was that scoped as homepage-only, or is it an oversight?
2. The chatbot's strongest reassurance line about pre-surgery anxiety is arguably the most emotionally important sentence on the site, and it is three taps deep in an opt-in widget most visitors never open. Should that reassurance live on the page itself?
3. Two floating get-help affordances occupy opposite bottom corners on every screen. Does that reduce friction toward booking, or does it split attention between two ways to avoid committing to the one real next step?
